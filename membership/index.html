Trader levels

Four planned discussion levels

These levels represent intended discussion categories and participation privileges. Access criteria and exact benefits will be finalised before launch.

Level 1

Starter

For traders building a solid base in XAUUSD and the community's way of working.

  • Learn the community framework.
  • Strengthen core XAUUSD knowledge.
  • Participate in foundational discussions.
  • Learn risk-management basics.
Level 2

Advanced

For traders with a method who want deeper analysis and structured comparison.

  • Participate in deeper chart-analysis discussions.
  • Compare structured trade plans.
  • Explore market structure, liquidity, and confluence.
  • Review trading journals and execution decisions.
Level 3

Pro

For traders who can explain a full process and help others improve.

  • Participate in advanced analytical discussions.
  • Share detailed trade theses and post-trade reviews.
  • Contribute thoughtful feedback and educational insights.
  • Help other members improve their analysis.
Level 4

Degen

A high-level category for experienced, experimental and unconventional perspectives.

  • Explore complex market scenarios and alternative theses.
  • Contribute rigorous trade breakdowns and critical analysis.
  • Emphasise accountability and transparent reasoning.
  • Disciplined risk controls are mandatory, not optional.
How advancement is planned to work

Earned through quality, not payment

Moving between levels should reflect how well you think, not how much you pay or one lucky trade. We intend to look at:

  • Analytical quality — clear theses with context, confluence and invalidation.
  • Risk awareness — consistent position sizing and respect for stops.
  • Transparent reviews — honest post-trade reviews, losses included.
  • Meaningful contributions — feedback that helps other members improve.

What membership aims to provide

  • Organised, level-aware XAUUSD discussion categories.
  • A place to post chart breakdowns, trade plans and post-trade reviews for feedback.
  • Exposure to multiple analytical frameworks applied to the same market.
  • A culture built on reasoning, risk discipline and honest review.

What it does not include

No guaranteed access to profitable traders, no winning signals, no financial returns or specific performance, and no personalised financial advice.

Membership FAQ

Can I pay now or pre-register?

No. Payment and member access are not available yet, and we’re not running a waiting list. Please don’t send money to anyone claiming to sell XAUUSD Global memberships.

Does a higher level cost more?

No. The plan is one membership at $19.99 per month. Levels are discussion categories and participation privileges earned through contribution quality — they cannot be bought.

How will I move between levels?

Advancement is planned to depend on demonstrated analytical quality, risk awareness, transparent reviews and meaningful contributions — not on payment or a single profitable trade. Exact criteria will be published before launch.

Is the price final?

$19.99/month is the currently planned price. It may change before launch, and the final price and terms will be shown clearly before anyone is asked to pay.

Will members receive trading signals?

No. Membership is about discussion, analysis and review. It does not include signals to copy, managed accounts or personalised financial advice.
