Contact XAUUSD Global

Questions about the website, a correction to an article, or questions about the community? Here's how to reach us.

How to reach us

Email us at info@xauusdglobal.com.

What we can help with

  • Content corrections. If you believe an article contains an error, tell us the page and the issue. We take accuracy seriously and will review it.
  • Website feedback. Broken links, accessibility problems or display issues.
  • Community questions. Anything about the community or the Founding 50 offer. Claim your free spot — the first 50 members get 3 months free.
  • Partnership or media enquiries.

What we can’t help with

  • Personal financial or trading advice. We can’t tell you whether to buy or sell gold, review your account, or recommend position sizes for your situation.
  • Broker disputes. Please contact your broker or the relevant regulator.

Beware of impersonation

Your safety matters to us: we never ask for money or wallet details by email or private message. Membership is only ever claimed through our membership page.
