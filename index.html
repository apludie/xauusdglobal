Sound familiar?

Surrounded by information. Still alone with the chart.

You've watched the videos and learned the concepts — structure, liquidity, order blocks, fair value gaps. Then you open your own chart and the same questions come back.

Is this a genuine breakout, or a liquidity sweep?

Why did this setup work yesterday but fail today?

Where exactly would my idea be wrong?

Is this a high-quality setup, or a trade I just want to take?

Who can review my analysis and tell me what I missed?

How do I stop relying on other people's calls?

Information is everywhere. Structured learning, honest feedback and accountability are much harder to find. That's the gap XAUUSD Global exists to close.

Our approach

Understand the idea, not just the entry.

Videos are a great way to discover concepts, and a trade idea can be useful. But understanding an idea is what lasts. When the trade fails or the market changes, reasoning is what lets you decide for yourself.

So every idea discussed at XAUUSD Global is expected to answer the same questions — before the outcome is known. Members learn to judge the quality of the reasoning, not just whether the trade won.

A well-reasoned trade can lose. A poorly reasoned trade can win. Learning to tell the difference is the skill we care about most.

FAQ

Common questions

Is this a signal group?

No. Trade ideas will be discussed, but always with their reasoning: context, thesis, invalidation and risk. The goal is for you to understand an idea well enough to judge it yourself, not to copy entries.

Who is XAUUSD Global for?

Mainly intermediate gold traders — people who know some concepts but want to apply them more consistently. Motivated learners and experienced traders who enjoy explaining their thinking are welcome too.

Is membership open?

Not yet. The guides on this site are free and available now. The community is being prepared and membership is planned at $19.99/month. Get launch updates to hear first when it opens — no payment and no obligation.

Will joining make me profitable?

No community, course or strategy can promise that. Trading leveraged gold products carries a high risk of loss. What we aim to help with is better reasoning, risk habits and review — the parts of trading you can control.

Why only gold?

Gold has its own drivers, sessions and behaviour. Focusing on one market lets discussions go deep and lets traders compare like-for-like analysis on the same chart.
