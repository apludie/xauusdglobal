## This week in one minute

- **US CPI ran hot.** September headline inflation rose **0.6%** on the month and **3.7%** on the year, up from 3.4%. Core inflation rose **0.3%** to **2.6%** a year, above forecasts. Energy prices jumped 5.2% in the month. ([BLS](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cpi.nr0.htm))
- **Gold dropped about 2%.** XAUUSD traded as high as about **$4,170** on the day, then fell through $4,100 to a five-day low of **$4,073**.
- **Fed hike odds jumped.** Markets moved from roughly **22%** to about **42%** odds of a rate hike at the October 28 meeting, and the 10-year Treasury yield sits above **5.2%**. ([FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/news/gold-crashes-below-4-100-as-hot-cpi-revives-october-fed-hike-bets-202610141757))

Gold had already slipped for three sessions before the report, with spot near **$4,143** on Wednesday morning. ([Mitrade](https://www.mitrade.com/au/insights/commodity/gold/gold-price-forecast-us-cpi-october-14-2026)) CPI simply added fuel.

## Why the CPI hit gold so hard

Gold pays no interest. When inflation runs hot, the market expects the Fed to keep rates higher for longer, or even raise them. That pushes **real yields** and the **US dollar** up, and both make gold less attractive to hold.

There is a twist this time. A big share of the inflation came from **energy**, driven by shipping risks around the Strait of Hormuz. Geopolitical stress usually *supports* gold as a safe haven, but the same stress is pushing oil and inflation higher, which keeps rates elevated. For now, rates are winning that tug of war.

:::note
**Pro tip:** when gold reacts to a data release, watch the US 2-year yield and the dollar index at the same time. If gold falls while yields rise, the move is about rates, and the next rate-sensitive event (here, the Fed) becomes the one to plan around.
:::

## Two scenarios traders are watching

**Scenario 1: $4,000 holds.** Oversold momentum, a quiet US calendar and safe-haven demand from the Middle East could bring buyers back at the round number. A reclaim of **$4,100–4,150** would show the CPI drop being absorbed, with **$4,200** the next test.

**Scenario 2: $4,000 breaks.** If yields keep climbing and hike odds rise further, a clean break of **$4,000** would put the **July lows** in play. Watch how price behaves at **$3,996 and $3,959**: a sweep and fast reclaim is a very different signal from steady acceptance below.

Either way, the real event is **Wednesday, October 28**: the FOMC decision at **2:00 pm ET (11:30 pm IST)** and the press conference at 2:30 pm ET. ([Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/2026-october.htm)) Expect gold to stay sensitive to any Fed speaker until then.
