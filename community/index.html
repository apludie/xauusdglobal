Community vision

A Global Community for XAUUSD Traders

We connect intermediate traders with advanced, Pro, and Degen traders to encourage better analysis, stronger execution habits, and continuous improvement.

In development · Registration not open yet
The mission

Stop learning alone. Start learning together.

Most traders improve slowly because they learn in isolation. They repeat the same errors, can't see their own blind spots, and rely on fragments of advice from social media.

XAUUSD Global is being built to change that for gold traders. Instead of selling information one way, we want to connect traders to traders — so an intermediate trader can put their analysis next to that of someone more experienced, ask why, and get an honest answer.

One market. Different perspectives. Better traders.

How discussions will work

Structured conversation, not a noisy feed

Chart breakdowns

Members post an XAUUSD chart with their read of structure, key levels and context.

Trade-plan reviews

Ideas are expected to include a thesis, an invalidation level and the planned risk — before the outcome.

Post-trade reviews

After the trade, members review the decision, execution and emotions — win or loss.

Level-based rooms

Discussion categories for Starter, Advanced, Pro and Degen keep conversations useful at every level.

Planned community standards

Principles we intend to hold members to

  • Explain the reasoning. Ideas without a thesis and invalidation don't belong in analysis threads.
  • Disclose risk. Talk about position size and risk in percentages, not just targets.
  • No paid signals or promotions. No selling signals, accounts or schemes to other members.
  • Review losses honestly. Mistakes are learning material, not something to hide.
  • Respect other perspectives. Challenge the analysis, never the person.
  • No guarantees. Nobody claims certainty about where gold goes next.
Membership does not guarantee profitable trading. The community is designed to improve analysis, discipline and review habits. It does not provide personalised financial advice, signals to follow, or any assurance of returns. Leveraged trading can result in substantial losses.
