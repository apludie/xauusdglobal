About XAUUSD Global

What is XAUUSD Global?

A gold-trading learning platform built on one idea: stop chasing signals and start understanding gold. Free XAUUSD guides are available now, and we are preparing a community where traders share their reasoning, get feedback and review their trades together.

Is XAUUSD Global a broker or signal service?

No. We don’t execute trades, hold funds, manage accounts or sell signals. We publish education and are building a discussion-based community.

Who writes the content?

Articles are published by the XAUUSD Global editorial team. Named author and reviewer profiles with verifiable backgrounds will be added as the project develops. Each article shows its publication date and when it was last reviewed.

Our approach

Is XAUUSD Global a signal group?

No. We don’t post trade calls to copy. We focus on the reasoning behind a trade idea — the market context, the evidence, what would prove it wrong, the risk and what can be learned afterwards — so you can build your own process.

Who is XAUUSD Global for?

Mainly intermediate gold traders: people who already know some concepts but struggle to apply them consistently. Motivated beginners and experienced traders who enjoy explaining their thinking are welcome too.

I'm a beginner. Is this too advanced for me?

No. Start with the free strategy hub and the risk-management guide. Every question is welcome — struggling is part of learning to trade, and nobody should feel foolish for asking.

What's free and what will be paid?

All guides, blog articles, the live price page and the news and calendar are free and will stay free. The planned membership adds the community — discussion, feedback and trade reviews. See what membership adds.

Will XAUUSD Global make me profitable?

No website, course or community can promise that, and we never will. Trading leveraged gold products carries a high risk of loss. We aim to help with what you can control: clearer reasoning, better risk habits and honest review.

Why focus on reasoning instead of entries?

An entry only works for one trade. Understanding why a setup exists, when it is invalid and how much to risk is something you can reuse on every chart. A well-reasoned trade can still lose — learning to judge decision quality, not just outcomes, is the skill that lasts.

Community and membership

Can I register now?

Not yet — but you can get launch updates. Add your email and we will let you know as soon as membership opens. Joining the list is free, creates no obligation, and you can unsubscribe at any time. There is no login or payment option yet.

How much will membership cost?

Membership is currently planned at $19.99 per month. This is a planned price and may change before launch. See membership and trader levels.

What are the Starter, Advanced, Pro and Degen levels?

They are planned discussion categories with different participation privileges. Advancement is intended to depend on demonstrated analytical quality, risk awareness, transparent reviews and helpful contributions — not on paying more or one profitable trade. Exact criteria will be finalised before launch.

What does 'Degen' mean here?

It’s a high-level category for experienced, experimental and unconventional trading perspectives. It is not an invitation to reckless trading: accountability, transparent reasoning and disciplined risk controls are central to it.

Will members get trade signals or guaranteed access to profitable traders?

No. The community is about sharing reasoning, analysis and reviews. We don’t promise signals, returns, specific performance or access to profitable traders.

Content and data

Is the gold price on your site live?

Price charts are provided by an embedded TradingView widget. Data may be real-time or delayed depending on the provider and is indicative only. We don’t generate, verify or store price data. See the XAUUSD price page.

Where do the news headlines come from?

Headlines on the news hub are collected automatically every hour from public RSS feeds — currently FXStreet, the Federal Reserve and the European Central Bank — and filtered for gold-relevant topics. We show the headline, a short excerpt, the source and the time, and link to the original article. We never invent or rewrite news. The economic calendar is provided by TradingView.

Are the chart examples real trades?

No. All examples and diagrams use hypothetical data and are labelled as illustrations.

Risk

Can following your strategy guides make me profitable?

No strategy or education guarantees results. Leveraged trading and CFDs can result in substantial losses. Our guides explain how traders analyse gold and manage risk; they are not personalised advice.

Where should a beginner start?

Start with what XAUUSD is, then risk management and position sizing, then market structure. Practise on a demo account before risking money.
