About XAUUSD Global

What is XAUUSD Global?

A worldwide community of gold traders. We connect XAUUSD traders from every corner of the world, teach on the live market with mentors and experienced traders, and help every member trade smarter. Free guides are available on this site right now.

Do you sell courses?

No. We don’t sell courses — we teach you on the live market. You learn by watching real gold price action with experienced traders, asking questions in real time and getting feedback on your own charts.

Who will I learn from?

Experienced gold traders, mentors and educators from around the world — plus a global network of members trading the same market. Many traders find their mentor right inside the community.

Who writes the content?

Articles are published by the XAUUSD Global editorial team. Each article shows its publication date.

Our approach

Is XAUUSD Global a signal group?

It’s much more than that. Trade ideas are shared with their full reasoning — the context, the evidence, the smart entry and exit, and the risk — so you understand every setup and can spot the next one yourself.

Who is XAUUSD Global for?

Gold traders at every level who want to grow faster: intermediate traders ready to become consistent, advanced traders sharpening their edge, and experienced traders who love sharing what they know.

I'm a beginner. Is this too advanced for me?

Not at all. Start with the free strategy hub, then step into the community where every question is welcome and experienced traders are happy to help you grow.

What's free and what's in membership?

All guides, blog articles, the live price page, the economic calendar and the trading calculator are free forever. Membership unlocks the community: live-market sessions, mentors, chart reviews, real-time trade ideas and traders worldwide. See everything membership includes.

Community and membership

How do I join?

Claim your spot with your email and confirm it. Your invitation to the community rooms arrives by email. See you in the community rooms soon!

What is the Founding 50 offer?

The first 50 members get the full XAUUSD Global community free for 3 months. After that, membership is $19.99/month. Once the 50 spots are taken, the offer closes.

How much is membership?

$19.99/month for everything — every room, every session, every level. The first 50 founding members get their first 3 months free. See membership and trader levels.

What are the Starter, Advanced, Pro and Degen levels?

They’re community rooms for every stage of your journey. You level up by sharing quality analysis, trading with discipline and helping other members — the more you contribute, the higher you climb.

What does 'Degen' mean here?

It’s the room for experienced, bold and creative traders exploring advanced setups and alternative ideas — with sharp reasoning and pro-level risk control.

Can I connect with traders from other countries?

Yes — that’s the heart of it. Traders from London, New York, Dubai, Mumbai, Singapore, Sydney and beyond meet in the same rooms, so there’s always someone watching gold with you.

Content and data

Is the gold price on your site live?

Price charts come from an embedded TradingView widget, so you get a professional chart right on the XAUUSD price page.

Where do the news headlines come from?

Headlines on the news hub are collected automatically every hour from public sources — including FXStreet, the Federal Reserve and the European Central Bank — and filtered for gold. Each links to the original article. The economic calendar is provided by TradingView.

Getting started

Where should I start today?

Start with what XAUUSD is, then risk management and position sizing, then market structure. Then claim your free founding spot and practise with the community.
