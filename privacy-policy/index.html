Privacy Policy (Draft)

We currently collect very little — and we want to be clear about exactly what happens when you visit.

Draft — not final. This policy is a draft describing the current static website. It must be reviewed by a qualified professional and updated before any personal data is collected (for example registration, newsletters, analytics or payments).

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Summary

  • We do not run sign-up forms, contact forms, accounts, newsletters or payments on this site.
  • We do not knowingly collect your name, email address, phone number, wallet address or payment details.
  • The site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. Some third-party services load when you visit, including cookieless analytics, as described below.

Who we are

XAUUSD Global operates the website at https://www.xauusdglobal.com/. Contact details are listed on our contact page once a monitored channel is available.

Data processed when you visit

Hosting (GitHub Pages)

The website is hosted by GitHub. Like most web hosts, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address and request details in server logs for security and operational purposes. Please refer to GitHub’s own privacy statement for details of its processing.

Fonts (Google Fonts)

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests these fonts, Google receives technical information such as your IP address and browser details. Please refer to Google’s privacy policy.

Charts and price widgets (TradingView)

Our price page and homepage price preview embed widgets provided by TradingView. These widgets load content from TradingView’s servers, which may receive technical information and may set cookies or use similar technologies according to TradingView’s own policies. The widgets load only when they come into view.

Local browser features

The site uses a small amount of JavaScript for navigation, filtering and widget loading. Neither our own code nor our analytics sets tracking cookies.

Analytics (Umami)

We use Umami Cloud, a privacy-focused analytics service, to understand how the website is used — for example which pages are visited, referring websites, browser and device type, and approximate country. According to Umami, it does not use cookies and does not collect personal information that identifies you. We use these statistics only in aggregate to improve the site. The script is loaded from Umami’s servers; please refer to Umami’s privacy policy for details of its processing.

Future registration and payments

Before community registration or payments open, we will publish an updated privacy policy describing what personal data will be collected, why, the legal basis, who processes it (for example authentication and payment providers), how long it is kept, international transfers, and your rights to access, correct or delete it.

Children

This website is intended for adults. Trading leveraged products is not appropriate for minors.

Changes

We will update this page when our data practices change and show the date of the latest update at the top.
