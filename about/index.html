About us

About XAUUSD Global

We are building a global home for gold traders who want to think more clearly, trade more carefully and improve together.

Purpose

Why XAUUSD Global exists

Gold is one of the most actively traded markets in the world, and one of the most unforgiving for underprepared traders. Its moves can be fast, news-driven and emotionally demanding.

Plenty of websites sell signals, and plenty of channels post screenshots of winning trades. Far fewer help traders understand why a setup made sense, how its risk was defined, and what to learn when it failed. XAUUSD Global exists to fill that gap with clear education today and a structured, global community tomorrow.

Vision

Where we're going

  • NowFree, original education on XAUUSD strategy, risk and market drivers.
  • NextCommunity registration, trader levels and structured discussion spaces.
  • LaterMember tools such as shared journals, review templates and session-based discussion rooms.

Plans may change as we learn from traders. We'll update this page as each phase becomes real.

Values

What we stand for

Learn together

Progress is faster when traders compare reasoning and give each other honest feedback.

Respect different perspectives

Structure, liquidity, session and macro traders can all read gold differently — and all learn from each other.

Explain the reasoning

An idea without a thesis and an invalidation level isn't analysis; it's a guess.

Disciplined risk management

Protecting capital comes before any target. Risk is part of every conversation.

Transparency about uncertainty

We are open about mistakes, losing trades and what we don't know.

Skill over profit promises

We build around skill development — never around unrealistic claims of returns.

Who is behind XAUUSD Global

XAUUSD Global is an independent project in its early stage. We'll introduce the people behind the site — with real names and verifiable backgrounds — on this page as the community develops. We won't publish invented credentials, trading records, testimonials or member numbers.

Our editorial standards

  • Articles are original, educational and written to explain concepts, not to sell outcomes.
  • Hypothetical examples are labelled as hypothetical. We do not present illustrations as real trades.
  • News is never invented. Headlines are collected automatically from public RSS feeds, always credited to the original publisher and linked to the source; we show only the headline and a short excerpt.
  • Each article shows its publication date and a “last reviewed” field that is only updated when a genuine review takes place.
  • We aren't a regulated financial adviser and nothing on this site is personalised advice. See our risk disclaimer.
